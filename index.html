how I work ↓

Script to final cut

  1. 01 · Script

    Research

    Talk to users, read the brief, map what people are really trying to do.

  2. 02 · Storyboard

    Flows

    Journeys and information architecture in Miro and FlowMapp.

  3. 03 · Shoot

    Design

    Wireframes, then high-fidelity screens and components in Figma.

  4. 04 · Edit

    Prototype & test

    Clickable prototypes, feedback, and cutting what doesn't earn its place.

  5. 05 · Release

    Hand off

    Specs and design-system notes so developers build what was designed.

about me ↓

The person behind the frame.

I studied design at NIFT Patna and have spent over four years designing client products at Wipro and now Capgemini, across retail, healthcare, travel and e-commerce. I've worked on AR interfaces, responsive web and design systems.

Outside work I shoot and edit videos. Framing a shot and framing a screen turn out to be the same problem: one subject, a clear path for the eye, and nothing that competes with it.
