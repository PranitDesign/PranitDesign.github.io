(03) — Process

How I work

Five stages, each with one job. Nothing gets designed before it's understood.

01

Discoverunderstand the people

Talk to users, read the brief and the data, and map what people are really trying to do. On Philips this meant personas, workshops and a full process map.

02

Definemap the journey

Journeys and information architecture in Miro and FlowMapp, so the whole story works before any pixel exists.

03

Designshape the interface

Wireframes, then high-fidelity screens and reusable components in Figma.

04

Validatetest and refine

Clickable prototypes and feedback. Anything that doesn't help the person gets cut.

05

Deliverhand off and support

Specs and design-system notes so developers build exactly what was designed.
